What Makes Raw Honey Different From Regular Honey

Not all honey is the same — and this matters for the ritual. Supermarket honey is typically pasteurized and filtered, which removes most of the enzymes, pollen, and trace compounds that make raw honey nutritionally distinct.

Raw, unprocessed honey — especially darker varieties like buckwheat — retains its full natural enzyme profile. These enzymes are part of what ancient traditions were working with, even if they didn’t have the biochemical vocabulary to describe them.

The second ingredient in the ritual also has its own ancient history. It appears by name in the Old Testament and has been used in traditional kitchens across the Middle East and Mediterranean for thousands of years. The video explains why these two specific ingredients, combined in the morning window, create a different effect than either would alone.
